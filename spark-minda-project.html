BOTTLENECK ANALYSIS

Bottlenecks & Inventory Signals

Key operational constraints identified during shop floor observations and utilization tracking.

CRITICAL

Porsche Housing RH

14 day Lead time, at 93% utilization. This is the primary drum operation for the entire plant line.

MONITOR

BorgWarner EAB LP

OEE bottlenecking at 73% utilization. Limited recovery margin for demand surges.

MONITOR

BorgWarner EAB HP

OP 30/40 and OP 50 together formed a 78% constraint, requiring coordinated scheduling to avoid WIP pile-up.

STABLE

Phinia 6 Cylinder

OP 10 machining managed at 63% utilization. Stable operation within capacity.

OEE SYSTEM

Tracking Machine Performance

Structured methodology for capturing shop floor metrics and translating them into actionable leadership dashboards.

01

Receive Daily Data

Collected breakdown details and efficiency figures from GDC and LPDC machine lines via shop floor supervisors.

02

Clean and Verify

Cross-checked discrepancies between operator logs and automated counters to ensure data integrity for OEE calculation.

03

Weekly Aggregation

Used custom Excel templates and pivot tables to compute weekly totals for Availability, Performance, and Quality.

04

Dashboard & Charts

Generated visual performance charts for plant managers to spot trends and assign resources to top bottleneck issues.

RECOMMENDATIONS

Actions for Production Stability

Strategic interventions proposed to protect flow, balance capacity, and institutionalize performance tracking.

01

Deploy Drum-Buffer-Rope

Pace GDC and gate cutting to the T6 capacity of 1800 units per day to prevent WIP build-up at heat treatment.

02

Implement Safety Stock

Maintain a minimum 3.5-day buffer at high-variation stages to protect downstream flow during machine downtime.

03

Formalize OEE Reporting

Standardize daily Excel-based daily dashboard entries across all 8 die-cast lines with mandatory weekly reviews.

04

Evaluate Parallel Lines

Assess future feasibility for BorgWarner LP/RP shared capacity if demand increases beyond 160 units/day.
