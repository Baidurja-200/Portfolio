— OEE SYSTEM

Tracking Machine Performance

01

Receive Daily Data

Collected breakdown metrics and efficiency figures from GDC and LPDC machine lines via shop floor supervisors.

02

Clean and Verify

Cross-checked discrepancies between operator logs and automated counters to ensure data integrity for OEE calculation.

03

Weekly Aggregation

Used custom Excel templates and pivot tables to compute weekly totals for Availability, Performance, and Quality.

04

Dashboard & Charts

Generated visual performance charts for plant managers to spot trends and assign resources to top bottleneck issues.

— RECOMMENDATIONS

Actions for Production Stability

01

Deploy Drum-Buffer-Rope

Pace GDC and gate cutting to the T6 capacity of 1800 units per day to prevent WIP build-up at heat treatment.

02

Implement Safety Stock

Maintain a minimum 3.5-day buffer at high-variation stages to protect downstream flow during machine downtime.

03

Formalize OEE Reporting

Standardize daily Excel-based daily dashboard entries across all 8 die-cast lines with mandatory weekly reviews.

04

Evaluate Parallel Lines

Assess future feasibility for BorgWarner LP/RP shared capacity if demand increases beyond 160 units/day.
