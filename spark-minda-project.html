Key Findings

Bottlenecks and inventory signals

Critical

Porsche Housing RH

T6 Heat Treatment operated at 93% utilization with a 44.7-hour lead time, making it the drum operation for the line.

High

BorgWarner EAD LP

OP 30/40 machining showed 73% utilization and limited recovery margin, indicating future parallel-line needs as demand grows.

High

BorgWarner EAD HP

OP 30/40 and OP 50 together formed a 71% constraint, requiring coordinated scheduling to avoid WIP pile-up.

Moderate

Phinia 6 Cylinder

OP 10 machining was manageable at 63% utilization, but any demand increase could push it toward constraint territory.

Recommendations

Actions proposed for production stability

R1

Deploy Drum-Buffer-Rope Scheduling

Pace GDC and gate cutting to T6 capacity of 208 units per day to prevent excess WIP and reduce lead time.

R2

Implement Stagewise Safety Stock

Deploy calculated minimum buffers at high-utilization stages to protect production continuity during breakdowns.

R3

Formalize OEE as an SOP

Institutionalize daily Excel dashboard entries and weekly leadership reviews of breakdown trends.

R4

Evaluate Parallel Lines

Assess parallel machine lines for high-utilization stages such as BW EAD LP OP 30/40 and Phinia OP 10.
